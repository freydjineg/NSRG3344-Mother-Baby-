📏 Med Math Conversion Reference (study before you start)
1 kg = 2.2 lb
1 lb = 16 oz
1 g = 1,000 mg
1 mg = 1,000 mcg
1 L = 1,000 mL
1 oz = 30 mL
1 tsp = 5 mL
1 tbsp = 15 mL (3 tsp)
1 cup = 8 oz = 240 mL
1 hr = 60 min
1 in = 2.54 cm
Diapers: 1 g = 1 mL urine
Moving the decimal: big unit → small unit, multiply (g → mg: ×1,000). Small unit → big unit, divide (mcg → mg: ÷1,000).
Formulas: Dose (mL) = desired ÷ have × volume · mL/hr = total mL ÷ hours · gtt/min = (mL × drop factor) ÷ minutes · Daily dose ÷ number of doses = per-dose amount.
Solving word problems: 1) Find what you're solving for (mL? gtt/min? mL/hr?). 2) Circle only the order, weight, and the concentration actually on hand. 3) Convert units FIRST (lb→kg, mg↔mcg, L→mL, min↔hr). 4) Round only at the end (hundredths for pediatric doses unless told otherwise; whole numbers for gtt/min). 5) Ask: does this answer make sense for a child this size?
